# Shadowless

**[Play it in your browser →](https://revbrd.github.io/shadowless/)**

A fan-made simulator of the original Pokémon Trading Card Game: the whole Wizards of the Coast
era, played to the letter of the original rules. Pick a starter deck, work your way up a ladder
of challengers, win booster packs, open them, and build decks from the cards you actually own.

The opponent is one AI at every rung. It plays every card by working out what each move is worth
across every way the coins could land. The ladder gets harder through the decks, not through
handicaps, and near the top it is genuinely hard to beat.

## Playing

A desktop browser and a mouse. Your collection saves in your browser.
